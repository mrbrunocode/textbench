Common questions

Does it handle decimals and negative numbers?

Yes — decimals (3.14) and negative numbers (-42) are both matched correctly.

What about numbers with commas, like "1,000"?

The comma breaks the match into "1" and "000" separately, since comma-formatted thousands separators aren't part of the number pattern. Remove thousands separators first if you need the full figure as one number.

Are duplicates removed?

No — every occurrence is listed, in order, since repeated numbers are often meaningful (e.g. the same total appearing twice).
