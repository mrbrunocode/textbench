Common questions

How does it count words?

It splits your text on any run of whitespace and counts the non-empty chunks, which matches how word-count works in most writing tools. Hyphenated words count as one.

Is my text uploaded anywhere?

No. All counting runs in your browser with JavaScript — nothing is sent to a server, so it works offline and there is nothing for us to store.

Is there a length limit?

No hard limit. Very large documents (hundreds of thousands of words) may slow down slightly because it recounts on every keystroke, but there is no cap.
