Common questions

Is the text sent to a server to generate the QR code?

No — the QR code library runs entirely in your browser (loaded once from a CDN, the same way this page's fonts are), and the encoding itself happens locally. Nothing you type is uploaded.

Is there a length limit?

QR codes have a real capacity limit that grows with the code's density — a short URL fits easily, but a very long block of text may fail to encode. If that happens, shorten the text or split it.

Can I scan this with any phone?

Yes — any modern phone's camera app reads standard QR codes directly, no separate scanner app needed.
